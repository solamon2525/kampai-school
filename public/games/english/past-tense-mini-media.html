📘 Past Tense Mini — สอนและฝึกสั้น

ป.4–6 · ภาษาอังกฤษ
was/were · กริยาปกติ -ed · went/saw/ate

was / were

I/he/she/it → was
you/we/they → were

She was happy.
They were at school.

Regular (-ed)

play → played
watch → watched
like → liked

Yesterday I played football.

Irregular

go → went
see → saw
eat → ate

We went home.
I saw a bird.
She ate rice.

Past tense = พูดถึงสิ่งที่เกิดขึ้นแล้ว (yesterday / last week / this morning)
